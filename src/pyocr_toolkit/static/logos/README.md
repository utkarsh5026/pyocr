# Brand logos

These SVGs are copied unmodified (apart from removing the `<title>` element) from
[Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org) v16.38.1, released under CC0 1.0:
`claude.svg`, `googlegemini.svg`, `googlecloud.svg`, `mistralai.svg`, `paddlepaddle.svg`.

The names and logos are trademarks of their respective owners. They are shown only to
identify which service or library a setting belongs to; their use does not imply endorsement.

Brands without an icon here (OpenAI, Microsoft Azure, AWS, Tesseract, EasyOCR, RapidOCR,
docTR, ...) are drawn as a lettered badge in `brands.py`. Several companies asked Simple
Icons to remove their marks, so don't add hand-made copies of those logos.
